No organization assigned

Your sign-in succeeded, but we couldn't place your account in an organization that IntelliStream DataHub recognises. Without a registered organization, we can't resolve a tenant for you, and you won't be able to access any data or features.

This usually means one of two things:

  • Your identity provider issued a token without an organization claim — your account may not yet be assigned to an organization in the directory.
  • Your token carries an organization that isn't registered as a tenant in this environment — this can happen if you've signed in to the wrong environment, or if the tenant hasn't been provisioned yet.

Please contact your administrator. If you believe this is a mistake, sign out and try again.
